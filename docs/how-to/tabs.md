# Work in tabs

Ctrl+T opens a tab with a conversation of its own, and `/nick` names its agent, which the tab then shows. Alt+1 and Alt+2 switch tabs. A tab behind the shown one keeps working, and its mark tells it is busy.

<!-- how-to: tabs -->

On [harness-multitool.dev](https://harness-multitool.dev/docs/how-to/tabs) this page plays it live (CON-187).

## In the real harness

- Ctrl+T a new tab, Ctrl+W close it, Alt+1..9 switch, or a click on a tab or `+`.
- `/nick <name>` names the tab's agent, which other agents reach by that name.
- The terminal's own tab title follows the active agent's nick (CON-170).
